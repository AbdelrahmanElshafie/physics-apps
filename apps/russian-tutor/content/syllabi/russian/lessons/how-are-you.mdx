"How are you?" is the first thing most people want after a greeting, and Russian has a
straightforward phrase for it. The complication isn't grammatical — it's that the question means
something slightly more literal than its English counterpart, and is answered accordingly.

## 1. Как дела?

<VocabCard word="Как дела?" stressed="Как дела́?" meaning="How are things? / How are you?" note="дела is the plural of дело ('matter, affair') — literally 'how [are] things', with no verb, per the zero-copula rule." />

Two close variants, differing only in formality — the same привет/здравствуйте split, and the same
ты/вы split from the conjugation lesson:

<Compare
  columns={['Russian', 'Used with']}
  rows={[
    ['Как дела?', 'Anyone — the default, works in most situations'],
    ['Как ты?', 'Someone you call ты — slightly more personal, asking about them rather than their affairs'],
    ['Как вы?', 'Someone you call вы — the formal equivalent'],
  ]}
/>

## 2. Answering

The answers are adverbs — the same -о forms you've already seen in хорошо́:

<Compare
  columns={['Answer', 'Means']}
  rows={[
    ['Отлично!', 'Excellent!'],
    ['Хорошо.', 'Good / well.'],
    ['Нормально.', 'Fine / normal / OK.'],
    ['Неплохо.', "Not bad. (не + плохо — the negation particle again)"],
    ['Плохо.', 'Bad.'],
  ]}
  caption="Нормально is worth noting: it's a genuinely common, perfectly positive answer, despite 'normal' sounding lukewarm translated literally into English."
/>

<Callout kind="warning" title="This is a real question, not a greeting formula">
In English, "How are you?" is often not a question at all — it's a greeting, and "Fine, thanks"
is the expected answer regardless of how you actually are. Как дела? is more literal: among
friends, people answer it honestly, including with Плохо and a reason. Answering a Russian friend
with a reflexive "Хорошо!" when things are clearly not fine can read as evasive rather than
polite. Among strangers or in formal settings, a brief positive answer is still normal.
</Callout>

## 3. Returning the question

<VocabCard word="А ты?" stressed="А ты?" meaning="And you? (informal)" note="А вы? is the formal version. А simply means 'and' here, used to pivot back to the other person." />

  Answer honestly. Among friends the question is meant literally, and a reflexive "fine" where
  things clearly aren't can come across as deflecting. The English habit of treating "How are
  you?" as a content-free greeting doesn't transfer.
</Predict>
