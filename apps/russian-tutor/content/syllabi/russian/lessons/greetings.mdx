---
title: Hello, goodbye, and thank you
summary: The handful of phrases that open and close almost every conversation in Russian.
readingMinutes: 10
---

A small number of fixed phrases cover most of what you say at the start and end of any
interaction. None of them need grammar yet — they're said as whole units, the way "thanks" or
"see you" are in English.

## 1. Hello

<VocabCard word="привет" stressed="приве́т" meaning="hi (informal)" note="Use it with friends, family, and people your own age — not with a stranger or someone older." />

<VocabCard word="здравствуйте" stressed="здра́вствуйте" meaning="hello (formal)" note="Longer and more careful to say, which is the point — it signals respect. Use it with strangers, elders, or anyone in a formal setting." />

<Callout kind="warning" title="There is no neutral middle ground">
Unlike English "hi" (which works almost everywhere), Russian greetings are split cleanly by
formality. Using привет with your boss or a stranger on the street can come across as too casual;
using здравствуйте with a close friend can sound oddly distant. When in doubt with someone you
don't know well, start formal.
</Callout>

## 2. Thank you, and you're welcome

<VocabCard word="спасибо" stressed="спаси́бо" meaning="thank you" />

<VocabCard word="пожалуйста" stressed="пожа́луйста" meaning="you're welcome / please" note="The same word serves both jobs — context tells them apart. After спасибо, it means 'you're welcome'; before a request, it means 'please.'" />

<Predict
  question="Someone says спасибо to you for holding a door. What's the standard reply?"
  options={['привет', 'пожалуйста', 'пока', 'извините']}
  answer={1}
>
  пожалуйста is the standard "you're welcome." привет is a greeting, пока is goodbye, and
  извините is an apology — each is a different fixed phrase for a different social moment.
</Predict>

## 3. Sorry, and excuse me

<VocabCard word="извините" stressed="извини́те" meaning="excuse me / sorry" note="The all-purpose polite apology — for bumping into someone, asking a stranger a question, or interrupting." />

## 4. Goodbye

<VocabCard word="до свидания" stressed="до свида́ния" meaning="goodbye (formal)" note="Literally 'until the meeting' — see you again." />
